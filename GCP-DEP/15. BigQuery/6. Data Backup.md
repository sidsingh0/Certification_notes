- Regional datasets, do not offer any replication.
- Multi-region datasets are automatically replicated across multiple-locations.
- Therefore they increase availability and disaster recovery
- However they are not meant to protect form user errors
	- In case user accidentally deletes a database, it will be deleted from all the locations.
	- Therefore we need to employ some techniques to prevent this
