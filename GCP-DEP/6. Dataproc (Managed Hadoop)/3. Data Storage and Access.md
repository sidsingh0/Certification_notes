- When we migrate our Hadoop/Spark to Dataproc
	- Jobs are migrated to cluster itself
	- The data is migrated to GCS
	- Jobs use Cloud Storage Connector to get the data
	- This avoids the need to replicate the data in HDFS (it is necessary in rare cases)
