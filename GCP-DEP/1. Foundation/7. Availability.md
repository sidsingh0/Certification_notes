## Regions & Zones
- Region is a large geographic area with multiple zones/data centers.
- Resources in the same region can communicate quickly and without costs.
- A zone is a datacenter/set of datacenter inside the region. 
- Spreading workload over multiple zones in the same region, improves fault tolerance
- Everything we deploy in GCP is tied to a region, zone or both. Example:
	- Cloud SQL: Tied to a zone
	- BigQuery: Tied to a region/multi-region
	- Storage: Tied to region/multi-region
	- Dataproc: Clusters are tied to zone
- We can assign our default zones/regions
- There maybe transfer costs for sending data between the regions
