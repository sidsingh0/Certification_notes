#### Throughput
- (more lanes = more cars simeltaneously)
- How much data a system can process at a time
- measured in GBPS
- 2 GBps is a good measure

#### Latency
- Delay between data ingestion and when it is made available for querying/processing
- Low latency means, once the data has been ingested, it is quickly available for use.
- Generally in few ms
