When we reach out to Google Support Team, they sometimes help us by accessing our environment.
In order to access this environment, they request for approvals.
The approver for this request should have `roles/accessapproval.approver`

# Groups
- Used to manage multiple users with similar permissions needs.
- Create a group in Google Workspace / Cloud Identity domain
- Add people to that group
- Register the group to IAM with required roles
- These roles are applied to all the members in the group
