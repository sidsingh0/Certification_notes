Network Address Translation
Allows VMs inside a VPC without a public IP, to access the internet(outbound)
Note: VM cannot access the internet without a public IP, NAT solves that

#### Benefits
- Reduces the attack surface, since the VMs dont have public IP
- Inbound internet is not allowed
- Scales automatically
- High availability/reliable
- Lower costs (Since lower number of public IPs are required)
